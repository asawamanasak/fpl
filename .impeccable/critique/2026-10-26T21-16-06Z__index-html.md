#### Priority Issues

- **[P0] Semantic Color Inversion Between Choice 1 (Micky) and Choice 2 (Gemini) Across Tabs**
  - **Why it matters**: Choice 1 is Sky Blue (`#38bdf8`) and Choice 2 is Emerald Green (`#10b981`) on Tabs 1–2, but on Tab 5 (`GW Review`) Choice 1 becomes Emerald Green (`.winner-c1`, `.val-c1`) and Choice 2 becomes Sky Blue (`.winner-c2`, `.val-c2`), causing users to misread who won each gameweek.
  - **Fix**: Define `--c1-accent: #38bdf8` and `--c2-accent: #10b981` in `:root` and enforce consistent manager color coding across Tabs 1, 2, and 5.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable colorize`

- **[P1] Sub-44px Touch Targets & Illegible Micro-Typography (`5.76px–10.8px`) on Mobile**
  - **Why it matters**: 956 `undersized-ui-text` warnings (`<11px`) make Thai vowel/tone marks and player metadata unreadable on `<400px` screens, while `.reset-btn` (~25px), `th.sortable-th` (~26px), `.h2h-row-action-btn` (~27px), `.gw-rev-mob-btn` (38px), `.mobile-toggle-btn` (40px), and `.tab-btn` (42px) fall short of the 44x44px touch target standard.
  - **Fix**: Raise all interactive controls to `min-height: 44px`, enforce an `11px` (`0.6875rem`) floor on functional text, declutter mobile `.starter-card` metadata, and embed compact club/pos/cost info inside the sticky Player column on mobile Tab 3.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable adapt`

- **[P1] Missing ARIA Tab Semantics, Keyboard-Inaccessible Sortable Headers, and Low Contrast on Benched Rows**
  - **Why it matters**: Zero `role="tablist"`/`role="tab"`/`aria-selected` attributes exist; all 35 `<th class="sortable-th">` elements lack `tabindex="0"`, `aria-sort`, and keyboard handlers; `.gw3-table tr.is-benched td { opacity: 0.68 }` drops text contrast to `3.7:1` (48 warnings); and `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` is missing.
  - **Fix**: Add WAI-ARIA tablist/tab/tabpanel semantics and URL hash state syncing, make `.sortable-th` keyboard-operable with `aria-sort`, replace `opacity: 0.68` on `.is-benched td` with explicit WCAG AA compliant muted text colors, and add `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable harden`

- **[P2] Information Redundancy, Card-in-Card Clutter, and Undefined `--bg-card-hover` Token in Tab 2 (`#tab-summary`)**
  - **Why it matters**: Tab 2 stacks 6 competing card frameworks (43+ sub-cards) that repeat the same insights 4 times, while `.chip-inventory-card` references an undefined `--bg-card-hover` token and 696 elements rely on inline `style="..."`.
  - **Fix**: Define `--bg-card-hover` in `:root`, consolidate Tab 2's xP and Solver sub-grids into a single Executive Comparison Table, align Pros & Cons row-by-row by topic, and wrap secondary reference tables (Chip Inventory, Multi-Week Roadmap) in collapsible `<details>` disclosures.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable distill`

- **[P2] AI-Slop Surface Artifacts (`dark-glow`, `border-accent-on-rounded`, `pulsing-dot`)**

  - **Fix**: Replace zero-offset neon glows with crisp 1px borders and neutral elevation shadows, replace `border-top: 3px` on rounded `.plan-column` cards with full 1px subtle borders, and make `.health-dot-active` static.
